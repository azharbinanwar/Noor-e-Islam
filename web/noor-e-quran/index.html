Noor e Quran

A calm place to read

Built for people who want to read more and be interrupted less. Fourteen reading themes, a page that remembers where you stopped, and nothing that leaves your device.

What it does

Read comfortably

Fourteen themes tuned for long sessions, from soft paper to warm amber and gentle dark. Adjust text size, line spacing and Arabic script.

Auto-scroll

Set the pace and read hands-free. Hold the page to pause, lift to carry on. Keep the screen awake while you read.

Keep your place

Bookmarks, notes, highlights in six colours, and collections. Resume takes you straight back.

Find any ayah

Full-text Arabic search, jump to a surah or verse, browse by surah or juz.

Share a verse

Studio turns any ayah into an image worth sending, saved to your gallery or shared directly.

Gentle reminders

Surah Al-Mulk at night, Surah Al-Kahf on Friday, and a daily reading reminder, all off until you ask.

Arabic and English

Fully translated into both, with proper right-to-left layout.

Questions

Does it need the internet?

No. The full Quran is inside the app; nothing is downloaded and nothing is uploaded.

Where are my bookmarks and notes stored?

On your phone only, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling removes them; there is no server copy to restore from.

Can I change the Arabic script?

Yes: Uthmani or IndoPak, with adjustable size and line spacing, from the reader's appearance sheet.

Which translations are included?

The first release ships the Arabic text; translations are planned and will arrive in an update.
