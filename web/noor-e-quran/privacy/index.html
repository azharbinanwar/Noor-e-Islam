Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Noor e Quran does not collect any personal data. There is no account to create, nothing is uploaded, and no information about you or your reading ever leaves your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-in, no user profile, and no analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any identifier that could be used to recognise you.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your reading data locally so it is there when you return. It stays in the app's private storage and is never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Bookmarks, notes, highlights and collections you create
  • Your last reading position, so you can resume where you left off
  • Preferences such as reading theme, text size, script, language and reminder times

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Because nothing is stored on a server, it cannot be recovered afterwards.

Permissions

Notifications

To show the reminders you switch on, such as Surah Al-Mulk, Surah Al-Kahf and the daily reading reminder. Reminders stay off until you enable them.

Alarms & reminders

So a reminder arrives at the time you set, rather than whenever the system next happens to wake the app.

Run at startup

To restore your scheduled reminders after the device restarts, which would otherwise clear them.

Reminders are generated entirely on your device. No reminder, and no information about which surah you are reading, is sent anywhere.

Saving an image

When you share an ayah as an image from Studio, the image is created on your device and saved to your gallery or passed to whichever app you choose to share it with. We never receive a copy.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking software, and no third-party accounts or services. Nothing is shared or sold, because nothing is collected in the first place.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised before that feature is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app are welcome at contact@kodeelite.com.
