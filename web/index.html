Noor e Quran

A calm place to read the Quran — with the tools to return to it, and nothing that follows you.

What it does

Read comfortably

Fourteen reading themes tuned for long sessions, with adjustable text size, line spacing and script.

Auto-scroll

Set the pace and read hands-free. Hold the page to pause, lift to carry on.

Keep your place

Bookmarks, notes, highlights and collections — and it resumes exactly where you left off.

Find any ayah

Full-text search, jump to a surah or verse, and search by reference or surah name.

Share a verse

Turn any ayah into an image worth sending, composed in Studio.

Gentle reminders

Surah Al-Mulk, Surah Al-Kahf on Friday, and a daily reading reminder — all optional, all off until you ask.

Private by design

Nothing leaves your device. There is no account, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics. Your bookmarks, notes and reading position stay on your phone, because there is nowhere else for them to go.

The Quran text

The Arabic text comes from the Tanzil Project, verified and used unmodified.
