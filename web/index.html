Noor e Islam

Prayer times, qibla, tracker and reminders, with the Quran built in

It finds your city, works out today's prayers on the device, and shows what is happening now with a countdown to the next. Everything else is one tap from the home screen.

Prayer times, anywhere

Any city, your method, Hanafi or Shafi‘i. Fajr to Isha with sunrise, Ishraq and Zawal.

Qibla

A compass that points to the Kaaba, with the distance to Makkah.

Prayer tracker

Mark each prayer, keep a streak, and see the whole month at a glance.

Reminders on time

A notification at each prayer, Jumu‘ah on Friday, dhikr and Quran when you want them.

Prayer Focus

Your phone goes quiet around each prayer and comes back by itself.

The full Quran

The same reader as Noor e Quran, with Studio to share an ayah as an image.

Noor e Quran

A calm place to read

Built for people who want to read more and be interrupted less. Fourteen reading themes, a page that remembers where you stopped, and nothing that leaves your device.

Read comfortably

Fourteen themes tuned for long sessions, with adjustable text, spacing and script.

Auto-scroll

Set the pace and read hands-free. Hold the page to pause, lift to carry on.

Keep your place

Bookmarks, notes, highlights and collections, and it resumes exactly where you left off.

Find any ayah

Full-text search, jump to a surah or verse, search by reference or name.

Share a verse

Studio turns any ayah into an image worth sending.

Gentle reminders

Surah Al-Mulk, Al-Kahf on Friday, and a daily reading reminder, all off until you ask.

Private by design

Nothing about you is stored anywhere. There is no account to make, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party service inside either app.

Noor e Islam uses your location on the device for prayer times and asks our own server only for your city's name. Noor e Quran never goes online at all.

  • Bookmarks, notes, highlights and your tracker stay on your phone
  • Every reminder is off until you turn it on
  • Quran text from the Tanzil Project, used unmodified
  • English, Arabic, Urdu and French, with proper right-to-left layout

About

Noor e Islam is made by KodeElite, a small independent studio. We wanted the apps we use every day for prayer and reading to be calm, honest and free of anything that watches: no account, no ads, no analytics, nothing to sell.

Noor e Islam is the main app. Noor e Quran is its reader on its own, for people who only want to read.

Questions, mistakes in a prayer time, or a feature you miss: write to contact@kodeelite.com. We read every message.
