Is it free?

Yes. Both apps are free, with no ads and no purchases.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Prayer times, the full Quran, azkar and everything else work completely offline once installed.

English, Urdu, Arabic and French, with Quran scripts in IndoPak Nastaleeq and Uthmani.
